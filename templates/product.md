# Product grounding

Every design judgement massimo makes in this repo is anchored here. Keep it short and true; update it when the product or its users change.

## Product

<One or two sentences: what the product is and the job it does.>

## Users

<Who uses it, what they are trying to get done, and what they are used to.>

## The bar

<Two or three products these users already trust, which this product should look and feel credible beside. Name them.>

## Brand feel

<Three or four words, and what to avoid.>

## Scope

- In scope to design: <surfaces, flows>
- Deliberately not built: <what the product does not do, so no review calls it "behind the market" for lacking it>

## Accessibility bar

<WCAG A or AA, and anything specific (keyboard-first, reduced motion, RTL).>

## Viewports

<e.g. desktop 1280, mobile 375. Which one leads.>

## Real content

<Where real data lives for mockups and stories: staging API, seed files, fixtures, CMS, a screenshot folder. Mockups never invent data.>

## Team

<Who designs, who builds, who approves. How decisions get made.>
